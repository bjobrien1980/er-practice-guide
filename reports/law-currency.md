## Load-bearing appellate authorities read directly

| Authority | Source and propositions checked |
|---|---|
| *Qantas Airways Ltd v Transport Workers' Union of Australia* [2023] HCA 27 | Official High Court judgment, also held in the Briefer High Court corpus. The Court held that s 340(1)(b) can be contravened where preventing the exercise of a workplace right is a substantial and operative reason even though the right did not exist at the time of the adverse action, where future exercise was expected. This is now cited directly in Module 25. |
| *Board of Bendigo Regional Institute of Technical and Further Education v Barclay* [2012] HCA 32 | Official High Court judgment, also held in the Briefer High Court corpus. The Court's treatment of the s 361 onus, direct decision-maker evidence, and the substantial-and-operative factor formulation has been checked against Module 27. |
| *Construction, Forestry, Mining and Energy Union v BHP Coal Pty Ltd* [2014] HCA 41 | Official High Court judgment held in the Briefer High Court corpus. Read for the controlling s 361 and reason-in-fact analysis and retained as a primary authority for the Module 27 reasoning. |
| *Qantas Airways Ltd v Transport Workers' Union of Australia* [2023] HCA 27 and the current Act | Read together to distinguish the future-exercise principle under s 340(1)(b) from the separate question whether a workplace right presently exists under s 341. |

## Changes made following the check

- Module 25 now cites *Qantas v TWU* [2023] HCA 27 directly for the future-exercise proposition and distinguishes that proposition from the present-existence question under s 341.
- Module 27 now states the s 360 multiple-reasons rule more precisely and records the limits of an independent review.
- Module 29 now includes the current Regulations source and qualifies its reg 3.01 summary by reference to the current Regulation rather than relying on *Shum* alone.
- Module 30 now presents the current s 351 attribute list, identifies the religious-institution exception and preserves the jurisdiction-specific current-law qualification.
- Module 33 now records the current Federal Court rules/forms sources and the 11 June 2026 Rules-change notice.

## Assurance limitation

The primary statutory and procedure sources above were checked directly. The module-level case lists still contain authorities marked as external or not independently read in the repository's pinpoint registry. A green pinpoint check confirms paragraph matching against the available source record; it does not independently verify every historical or recent judgment, later appeal, or current proposition. The remaining case-specific limitations are retained in the modules' `Decisions cited` sections and must be resolved before treating every cited case as independently assured.
